WHAT 008 FIXES — statement reconciliation
================================================================
THE DEFECT

  The engine read a statement's summary block by guessing which printed
  figures were credits and which were debits from a list of category names,
  and never checked the answer. On the supplied 61-document batch that
  produced a summary that did not add up on 23 of the 24 documents where all
  four figures were found — silently, because nothing tested it.

  Reported before the fix:
    reconciliation_not_possible   43 documents
    reconciliation_mismatch        4 documents
    reconciles = true              1 document

THE FIX

  src/scanner/engine/balanceEquation.js reads the signs the bank itself
  printed, and where a sign is missing solves for the one assignment that
  reproduces the printed ending balance. A reading is reported as verified
  only when the arithmetic closes to the cent.

  Ten per-bank summary adapters are replaced by that one module. What remains
  beside it is genuinely different: online-banking activity printouts (which
  print no equation), OCR pattern recovery, and summing dated deposit rows.

  Also fixed, each found while measuring the above:
    - Reconciliation no longer depends on reading every page. The summary is
      on page one; only the transaction-row check needs the whole statement.
    - A part-read statement reports the bank's own deposit total as revenue
      instead of a share of the month (deposits.trueRevenueSource says which).
    - An application is judged on application wording, not on bank-statement
      wording it was never going to contain.
    - A statement whose arithmetic adds up is trusted whatever wording it
      uses, which rescues two-column summaries.
    - A holder row that ran into the marketing column beside it still yields
      the business name ("ABBASPOUR INC IMCU now offers Visa credit cards.").
    - An address line is an address even when a suffix follows it on the row.
    - deposits.totalDeposits read a key that did not exist.
    - A money figure written $2,000.00 was discarded as a year.
    - coreUtils.labeledMoney had no callers left and was removed.

INDEPENDENT CHECK — month-to-month continuity
  Across the batch, every statement's closing balance equals the next
  statement's opening balance for the same account, exactly, in all 12
  multi-month company folders. Nothing in the engine compares statements to
  each other, so this is independent evidence that the figures are right.

THE ONE SETTING WORTH YOUR DECISION
  The approved default reads 3 pages of each statement. That is why 43 of 61
  documents are marked "read in part" and sent to review, even though their
  printed summary reconciles. Measured on the same 61 files, same build:

                          3 pages (default)   every page
    Completed                    14               50
    Needs review                 47               11
    Read in part                 43                6
    Average score              94.3%            94.3%
    Runtime                    59.2 s           68.7 s

  Reading every page costs about 9 seconds across 61 files and clears 34
  documents out of review. The headline figures do not change, because they
  come from the printed summary either way; what the extra pages add is the
  transaction detail — MCA positions, NSF counts, daily balance.

  This was NOT changed. "Regular scan default: 3 pages" is an approved rule.

NOT VERIFIED
================================================================
  - 12-December Abbaspour.pdf. OCR did not recover the row carrying its
    opening balance, so its arithmetic cannot be proved. The row is present
    in the PDF image; the text is not. Reported as such, not guessed.
  - The other 58 files of the original 119-file batch. They were not supplied
    to this session.
  - Real CRM lead sending. It requires the host CRM `onSendLeads` callback.
  - Freeform routing beyond the built-in deterministic patterns. It requires
    the optional host CRM `routingInterpreter` callback.
  - Electron packaging itself. The renderer module is prepared for it, but no
    Electron shell was built or run here.
  - A `file://` origin. Chromium blocks Web Workers on `file://`, so the
    browser suites serve dist/ over a local app origin (http://scanner.localhost)
    that stands in for Electron's local app protocol. No server runs and no
    network request is made.

    the name of 2 EVERFRESH MARKET INC. The scanner reads the holder name off
