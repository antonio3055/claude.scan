THREE DEFECTS FOUND WHEN THE WHOLE DOCUMENT WAS FINALLY READ, AND FIXED
================================================================
  Reading more pages surfaced a document that used to score clean only
  because its debit section, past the old page cap, was never reached:
  "goodcents pest controlnk -compressed-protected.pdf" (12 pages, batch 2).
  With the full document read it came back as a false reconciliation
  MISMATCH, difference $66,755.85 — a claim that the bank's own printed
  summary is wrong. Tracing it down found three separate bugs:

  1. balanceEquation.js zeroed every deposit/withdrawal component whose
     sign could not be proven exactly, instead of reporting its closest
     reading. This document's real debit total, $75,266.74, was reported
     as $0. Fixed with closestSigns(): signs already fixed by print or
     keyword are kept, and the remaining ones are chosen to come closest
     to the printed ending — never marked verified, but no longer zeroed.

  2. transactionParser.js recognised "Electronic Deposits" and "Electronic
     Withdrawals/Payments" as section headings, but not "Electronic
     Credits" / "Electronic Debits" — the exact heading this document
     used for its 60 transaction rows, all misread as credits with $0 in
     debits as a result. Fixed by widening both section regexes.

  3. reconciliation.js discarded an existing, real but UNVERIFIED
     statement-summary equation (difference $23.68) in favour of this
     scanner's own row-by-row transaction totals — and across 211 heavily
     OCR'd debit line items spanning several pages, that row-level total
     was far worse (difference $66,755.85). Added a new outcome,
     reconciles: null, reason: 'equation_not_provable': an unproven
     reading straight from the statement's own printed summary is
     stronger evidence than hundreds of reconstructed OCR'd rows, and is
     now reported honestly as "cannot be proven" rather than forced to a
     false pass or reported as a contradiction the evidence doesn't
     support.

  Verified on the real document, before and after all three fixes:

    deposits         $0              -> $76,403.68
    withdrawals       $0              -> $75,266.74
    reconciliation    false, mismatch -> null, unproven
    difference        $66,755.85      -> $23.68

  Six new tests added: 3 in test-balance.mjs, 3 in test-transactions.mjs.
  No existing test was contradicted — every reconcile() call site was
  checked, and none passes a non-null, unverified equation that the new
  branch would now intercept.
