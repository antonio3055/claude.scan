WHAT WAS ADDED
================================================================
Every statement now carries `statementIdentity`: the holder name exactly as the
bank printed it, any DBA on the statement, and the address split into street,
town, state and postcode.

Every lead now carries `companyInfo`: the application's name, DBA and address,
the distinct values read off the statements, and three flags saying which of the
three differ. The extraction panel shows both, side by side, under "On the
statements", with the differing ones marked.

Neither source overwrites the other. That is the point: a business is often
banked under a name that is not the one on its incorporation, and the bank often
holds an address the application has moved on from.

HOW THE ADDRESS IS READ, AND WHAT IT REFUSES TO READ
================================================================
  The address sits under the holder's name but rarely on its own: the bank sets
  its own address and phone numbers in a column beside it, and rebuilding the
  rows interleaves the two. Rows are not counted — the first street after the
  name is taken, then the first town after that street, which steps over the
  bank's column without knowing anything about the bank.

  Checked in the real browser and in the engine suite:
    - the bank's own town is never taken as the holder's
    - marketing copy after the street is cut at the street type
      ("2998 SCOTT BLVD reserve your seat by calling..." -> "2998 SCOTT BLVD")
    - a town row carrying the bank's column is still read
      ("CLEVELAND OH 44127-1831 Online: wellsfargo.com")
    - a mail-sort code before the house number is dropped
    - no address is invented when none is printed

================================================================
SAME PLACE, DIFFERENT WORDS
================================================================
  An application says "1201 Richardson Drive, Richardson, TX 75080"; the bank
  writes to "1201 RICHARDSON DR STE 180, RICHARDSON, TX 75080-4610". One address
  written twice. Reporting that as a difference would bury the ones that really
  are different, so addresses are compared on the house number with the
  postcode, not on the words.

  Names are compared the other way — as printed. "Datalab Infotech Corporation"
  and "DATALAB INFOTECH INC" are one company for grouping, and exactly the
  difference worth showing.

TWO NAME DEFECTS FIXED ALONGSIDE
================================================================
  - "ESTAFFLLC4812 ESTAFF LLC": the account number fused onto the name in the
    routing block is dropped — but only when the code restates the name, so
    "23HUNDRED VENTURES INC", "366 Metro Mart INC", "984 LLC" and
    "4Bs Entertainment LLC" all keep their leading characters. The first attempt
    at this rule stripped "23HUNDRED" and was caught by re-running the first
    batch; the rule was tightened rather than shipped.
  - "04-13-2026 ZEL FROM AL MAWA LLC": a row opening with a posting date is a
    transaction, not the account holder, and is no longer read as a company.
